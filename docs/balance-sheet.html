Protocol. For each archetype a fixed sparring cast is drawn from that same archetype — two allies, three opponents, the median-power members of the bucket. Every hero is dropped into the same fight in place of one ally, so the only thing that changes between runs is the hero under test. Runs are seeded: a difference between two heroes is a difference in the heroes.

Reading poison. Poison is the only damage that skips the DEF curve — no damage formula, no mitigation, no dodge, no crit. At this max-level premise a front-liner sits near DEF 2483, so 100 raw direct damage lands as 10.8 while 100 poison lands as 100. A hero leaning on the poison column is banking on an exemption worth 9.3× here and about 2× for a normal Lv 30 team.
